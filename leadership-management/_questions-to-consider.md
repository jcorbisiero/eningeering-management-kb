# Leadership and Management — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Your actual impact

- What would my team do differently if I weren't watching?
- What would my leadership look like to someone who couldn't see my intentions — only my actions?
- When did I last change my mind because of something a team member said?

## Organizational risk

- Which organizational risks am I aware of that I haven't acted on?
- What am I protecting my team from that they should actually be dealing with?
- What is the longest time horizon I'm actively planning against — and is that long enough?

## Looking ahead

- What would I need to let go of to operate at the next level of leadership?
- If my team could give me one piece of feedback anonymously right now, what would it most likely be?
- What leader am I trying to become, and what would it look like if I were already there?
