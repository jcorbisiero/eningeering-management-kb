### Ownership and Agency

Strong ownership means understanding the architecture, product requirements, tradeoffs, and long-term direction of a system. It means reviewing the work produced by agents and making decisions confidently.

Agency is the ability to iterate without permission

* You still have to get your work done  
  * Do not abandon your responsibilities to take on something when you’re uncertain of the value  
* Establish your credibility first  
  * For juniors, people want to see that you can consistently handle the work already in front of you  
  * The more trust you build, the more room you’ll usually get to explore your own ideas  
  * Good leaders want to keep employees energized and excited  
* Validate the idea before going too far  
  * If you’re still early in your career, go and chat with more senior members on your team when you have an idea  
  * Sometimes a quick conversation with your manager or a senior engineer is enough to find out whether the idea you have is valuable or if you’re missing context. Maybe your manager has a plan to solve the problem you think is important. Maybe they are waiting for it to become a big enough problem that they can actually get budget to fix it rather than continually putting Band-Aids on it.  
* Try to connect your ideas to the actual business  
  * “I think this would be interesting to build” vs “This would help the business”  
  * Take the time to actually learn what the business needs and then connect that to your ideas  
* Don’t just use agency as an excuse to escape the work you’re supposed to be doing  
  * The goal isn’t to find more interesting work while ignoring everything else
