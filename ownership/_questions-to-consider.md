# Ownership — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Agency and accountability

- Where am I waiting for permission I could simply take?
- Which outcomes on my list am I accountable for versus merely responsible for — and do I act accordingly?
- When something goes wrong in my domain, do I investigate or explain?

## Creating vs. bottlenecking ownership

- Where am I creating dependency on myself instead of building system-level ownership?
- Which parts of my work do I hand off too early because they're uncomfortable?
- What would change if I treated every outcome in my domain as fully mine?

## Looking ahead

- What area of my work has the most unclear ownership — and what am I doing about it?
- Where is a lack of ownership on my team creating invisible risk?
- What would have to be true for the people around me to feel the same level of ownership I do?
