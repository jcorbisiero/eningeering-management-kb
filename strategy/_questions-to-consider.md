# Strategy — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Clarity of direction

- If I had to defend my current roadmap to a well-informed skeptic, where would I struggle?
- What is my team optimizing for that the broader organization doesn't actually care about?
- What bet am I making right now that I haven't made explicit — to myself or to my team?

## Assumptions and risk

- What would have to be true for my current strategy to fail — and how likely is that?
- What am I treating as a constraint that is actually a choice?
- Where is the team solving a problem with execution that actually requires a strategic rethink?

## Adaptation

- When did my team last change direction based on new information — and was that fast enough?
- What would I do differently if I had half the resources and twice the pressure to show results?
- What am I avoiding making a decision on because the answer might require an uncomfortable change?
