# Engineering Strategy Template

## Context
Context section should share in which context the Engineering Strategy started. The main trigger event that caused this document to be written.

Some of the most common moments to start an Engineering Strategy can be:

- As part of the next Quarter Planning, it should contain an Engineering Strategy.
- Leadership/Team/Department detected some critical areas of improvement or risks and requires a major effort to address the high-stake business problem.
- New Project/Product/Initiative Started and should contain an Engineering Strategy.
- The Product reached Product Market Fit stage and now we need to thinking on growing the product, which will require several changes on the Engineering side like Architecture and Methodologies

# High stakes business problem/challenge
The high-stake business problem/challenge is what we need to address as Engineering. It should be business centric instead of engineering.

# 🤔 Understanding
The understanding phase is about **providing visibility** on the areas we consider can directly impact our high-stake business problem.

# Direction

Now that we understand where we are.
The Direction is our **guideline to overcome our high-stake business problem**. It helps us to stay aligned and be aware of what we should do, and, more importantly, **what we shouldn’t do.**

Therefore**, **the Direction** must be **focused** and heavy cohesive.

# Key Results

# Coherent Actions
Now we know where we are and where we want to be.

Coherent Actions are the steps that help us to navigate toward the goal.

They are aligned with the Direction and can be accomplished incrementally. Measuring the progress and gaining shorter feedback loops to understand if we are going in the right direction.
