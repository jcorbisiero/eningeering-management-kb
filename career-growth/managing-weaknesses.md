# Managing Weaknesses

* Weakness — something that gets in the way of your success  
* Identifying weakness begins with self-awareness  
* You can't manage a weakness from a deficit. A person's natural reservoir of identified talent is the only true resource they have for neutralizing their weaknesses.  
* Think through how they can apply their strengths to manage their weaknesses.  
* For example, you can ask them questions, such as:  
  * How can you use your competitive nature to be a better listener?  
  * How will you use your relationship strengths to improve the quality of execution?  
  * How can your talent for analytical thinking help you influence others?  
* By leveraging their natural talents and strengths, they are better equipped to manage and compensate for their weaknesses.
