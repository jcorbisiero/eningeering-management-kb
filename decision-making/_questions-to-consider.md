# Decision-Making — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Your decision process

- When did I last revisit a decision I was confident about — and what did I find?
- Am I optimizing for making good decisions, or for being seen as decisive?
- What information would meaningfully change the decision I'm about to make?

## Delegation and ownership

- Which decisions am I making that should be delegated to someone closer to the problem?
- Where am I bottlenecking my team's decision-making by requiring my sign-off?
- What decisions is my team avoiding escalating to me that I should be making?

## Learning from outcomes

- When a decision goes badly, do I examine the process or just the outcome?
- What decision anti-patterns show up most often in my team's retrospectives or postmortems?
- What decision from the last six months am I most uncertain about in hindsight — and why?
