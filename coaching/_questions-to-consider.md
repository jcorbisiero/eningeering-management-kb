# Coaching — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Your coaching instincts

- When I last coached someone, was I helping them solve their problem — or solving it for them?
- What assumptions do I make about what someone needs before they finish explaining the situation?
- Do I adjust my coaching approach when it isn't working, or do I push harder on the same approach?

## Your impact on others

- Which team members am I over-coaching? Which am I underinvesting in?
- Am I creating capability in the people I work with, or dependency on me?
- What would it look like to coach someone toward an outcome I personally disagree with?

## Looking ahead

- If I stopped coaching someone tomorrow, could they continue making progress on their own?
- What type of coaching conversation do I consistently avoid — and why?
- Who has benefited most from my coaching, and what made those engagements different?
