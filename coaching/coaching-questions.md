# Coaching Questions
Coaching questions turn attention to the person.

* What did you notice about yourself in that meeting?
* What was going on for you when she said that?
* What do you make of your reaction?

### Steps
* Event: “What happened?”
* Meaning: “What do you make of what happened?”
* Insight: “What does this reveal about you?”
* Growth: “What does this insight suggest for you?”

### Example Questions
Example 1
* They say, “My boss micromanages.”
* You say, “So your boss is up in your business. What does his style bring out in you?”

Example 2
* They say, “People aren’t accountable.”
* You say, “You want people to be accountable. How might you be making it easier for people to avoid responsibility?”

Example 3
* They say, “I need to delegate more.”
* You ask, “What makes holding on attractive to you?”
