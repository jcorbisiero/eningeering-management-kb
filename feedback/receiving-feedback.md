# Receiving Feedback

## Default posture

Find out more about what caused the person to say what they said before reacting or defending.

## Phrases that work

**To invite more context:**
> "Ooh, that's very helpful. Thank you so much for bringing this up. I want to make sure I understand and can work on the right things. Could you share a bit more about [the feedback]?"

**To get a concrete example:**
> "This feedback is really helpful. [Mention why it's helpful.] Do you have an example that comes to mind? / When have you noticed that I tend to do this? This will help me know when to be more aware of this."

## The key principle

The way people perceive you is just as important as your original intent. How something lands is part of the signal — not a distortion of it.
