# Feedback — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Giving feedback

- What feedback am I withholding because the timing never feels quite right?
- When I give feedback, am I describing behavior and impact — or character and intent?
- Am I giving feedback that's honest, or feedback that preserves the relationship at the expense of truth?

## Receiving feedback

- What feedback about myself have I been slow to act on — and what's the actual reason for the delay?
- What uncomfortable feedback have I received more than once and not yet integrated?
- When was the last time I asked for feedback on how I give feedback?

## The feedback culture around you

- Do the people I manage have a clear picture of where they stand right now — not last quarter, right now?
- What would have to change for people on my team to give me more direct feedback?
- Where is feedback on my team flowing freely — and where is it getting stuck?
