# The Most Valuable Question

**"On a scale of 1 to 10, how would you rate …?"**

Apply it to anything: project progress, team motivation, your last presentation, the onboarding experience.

Any answer less than a 10 always gets this follow-up:

**"What would make it a 10?"**

The follow-up does the work. It shifts the conversation from evaluation to action, invites the other person to name the specific gap, and gives you something concrete to act on.
