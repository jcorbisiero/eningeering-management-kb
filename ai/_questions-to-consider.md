# AI — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Your assumptions and habits

- What assumptions am I making about AI capabilities that could become outdated within a year?
- Which parts of my workflow am I resisting automating — and is that resistance principled or just habitual?
- Am I building skills that will compound alongside AI, or skills that AI will gradually replace?

## Your team and organization

- How would I evaluate whether AI is actually improving my team's output, versus just changing its texture?
- What signals would tell me that AI adoption is creating hidden risk — in quality, ownership, or judgment — on my team?
- If AI eliminated 50% of the tasks on my current roadmap, what would I focus on instead?

## Looking ahead

- What would it mean for my role if AI makes individual contributors significantly more leveraged in the next two years?
- Which AI-related decisions am I deferring that will be harder to make later?
- What am I not measuring today that I would regret not having data on a year from now?
