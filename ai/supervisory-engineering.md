# Supervisory Engineering Work

- Most of software engineering is on a path to becoming automated, it isn't the same job any more.
- We may still call it "software engineering", but the role is changing and requires new skills, new training, new definitions of what good looks like, and new ways of hiring and growing people

The next step must be to start redesigning the job and the system. This means:

- Deliberately choose the level of automation to operate at, and for which types of work: deciding this helps us understand what we expect the human to do.
- Design system signals to support situation awareness: build our harnesses and software factories to surface just the right signals, at the right time, in the right way and at the right level of fidelity to support our comprehension of the state of the system without needing to "review harder".
- Describe intervention requirements and mechanisms: explicitly set expectations about when and how humans should step in (from small adjustments to taking over) and what they're accountable for, then ensure the system is designed to support that.
- Prepare people for the job: invest in the skills and training that supervision demands, especially for the rare moments when they need to step in.
