# Hiring and Interviews — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Bias and blind spots

- What biases might be shaping my read on candidates I instinctively like or dislike?
- If I were interviewed using our current rubric, would I be hired?
- Which candidates are we consistently passing on — and is that pattern defensible?

## What the process actually selects for

- What does our interview process reward that isn't what we actually need on the team?
- What skills am I hiring for that the team already has in abundance?
- What signals in a resume or conversation have I learned to trust — and have I validated them against outcomes?

## Looking ahead

- Who is declining our offers, and what would explain that pattern?
- If I could redesign our hiring process from scratch knowing what I know now, what would I change first?
- What bar am I holding candidates to that I don't hold myself or existing team members to?
