# Communication — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Clarity and intent

- What is the gap between what I intended to communicate and what was actually received in a recent exchange?
- When was the last time I simplified a message instead of adding more context?
- What am I over-explaining because I'm uncertain, rather than because the topic is genuinely complex?

## Audience and medium

- Who on my stakeholder list gets the least of my communication effort — and is that the right call?
- Where do I default to async communication when synchronous would be faster, or vice versa?
- Which of my communication habits serve the reader, and which serve my own anxiety?

## Looking ahead

- What important message have I been putting off sending because the framing isn't perfect yet?
- If I could only communicate one thing to my team this week, what would it be — and have I actually said it?
- What feedback have I received about how I communicate that I haven't acted on?
