# Ask More Questions

- A small misunderstanding early can turn into a big misunderstanding later, because all the things you misinterpret based on the first misunderstanding will become misunderstandings in their own right, and so on
- Simetimes people don’t ask questions because they’re not really trying to understand. They’re happy to just trust that the person they’re talking to knows what’s going on
    - This especially happens when that person is a well-respected or more senior engineer
- However, once you have skin in the game, that all changes
    - When you’re in a position where you are the one responsible for success or failure of a project you will find yourself wanting to ask many questions.
    - Success or failure here means where you are going to have to go away after the conversation and take a bunch of concrete actions

## Call to Action
- You should all be asking way more questions
- You should all be trying harder to actually understand what you’re hearing
- Don’t trust that the person (or AI model) you’re talking to knows what they’re doing, even if you think they’re smarter than you
- Nobody understands complex software products
