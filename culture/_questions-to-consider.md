# Culture — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## What you're modeling

- What behaviors on my team do I tolerate that I would not explicitly endorse?
- What cultural pattern am I personally modeling versus merely advocating for?
- If a new hire joined today, what implicit norms would they absorb in the first two weeks?

## Hidden forces

- What incentive structures are silently shaping behavior in ways I haven't fully examined?
- Where does psychological safety exist on my team — and where is it absent?
- What would a candid culture audit of my team reveal that I'd be uncomfortable sharing?

## Looking ahead

- What cultural debt is accumulating that I'm aware of but haven't prioritized addressing?
- Which cultural norms that made sense at an earlier stage are now holding the team back?
- If I left tomorrow, which parts of the culture I care about would survive — and which wouldn't?
