# Software Engineering — Questions to Consider

Use these questions when exploring the notes in this folder. They are not meant to be answered all at once — pick one or two that create friction.

## Honest assessment of the system

- What technical debt am I aware of that no one is currently talking about?
- If I inherited this codebase today, what would frustrate me most?
- What would I design differently today, knowing what I know now?

## Quality and practice

- Which engineering practices does my team follow because they genuinely help — versus out of habit or ceremony?
- What quality signals am I ignoring because addressing them would slow down delivery?
- When a production bug happens, do I find myself explaining why it's acceptable — or asking what made it possible?

## Looking ahead

- What architectural decision from the past is becoming the most expensive constraint today?
- Where is accidental complexity accumulating that no one has the mandate or time to address?
- What would it look like if the team held itself to a higher bar — and what's stopping that?
